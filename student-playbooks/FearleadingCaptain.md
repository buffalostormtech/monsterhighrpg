### Fearleading Captain Moves

- ☐ **Cheer Them On** (Heart)  
  When you rally someone mid-fight or mid-scene, roll +Heart.  
  - On a 10+: they gain +1 to all rolls for their next turn.  
  - On a 7–9: they gain +1 to a single roll, or may reroll a failed save.  
  - On a 6 or less: they still get +1, but you take a Condition (Tired, Frazzled, Embarrassed, etc.).

- ☐ **Squad Goals** (Style)  
  When you invoke your squad status to sway someone, roll +Style.  
  - On a 10+: NPCs are impressed, intimidated, or eager to help.  
  - On a 7–9: they go along with it, but demand a favor or expect a show.  
  - On a 6 or less: they mock you or expose a weakness in front of others.

- ☐ **Strike a Pose** (Heart)  
  When you enter a scene dramatically, roll +Heart.  
  - On a 10+: everyone takes notice and your team gains +1 forward.  
  - On a 7–9: you get noticed, but so do your enemies.  
  - On a 6 or less: you trip, overdo it, or provoke jealousy.

- ☐ **Hold the Formation** (Brains)  
  When you work as a unit with someone, roll +Brains.  
  - On a 10+: you both act as one — take +1 AC or advantage on attacks this round.  
  - On a 7–9: only one of you gets the benefit.  
  - On a 6 or less: you clash mid-move; the GM describes a misstep or miscommunication.

## Combat Block (d20 System) :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}  

**Primary Combat Stat:** Heart or Style  
**HP:** Moderate  
**EP Gain:** +1 per 2 levels  
**Save DC:** `8 + proficiency + Heart or Style modifier`

### Fearleading Captain Powers

- ☐ **Motivational Shout** – *1 EP, Bonus Action*  
  Choose up to 2 allies within 30 ft. They each gain:  
  - +1d4 to their next attack roll or saving throw, and  
  - Temporary HP equal to your Heart modifier.

- ☐ **Formation Smash** – *1 EP, Action*  
  Choose an ally adjacent to you. You both make a single attack against the same target.  
  - If both hit, the target must make a Guts Save or be knocked prone.

- ☐ **Squad Rally Zone** – *2 EP, Cooldown 3 turns*  
  You create a 15-ft aura around you for 1 minute. Allies inside gain:  
  - +2 AC vs ranged attacks, and  
  - May reroll one failed Heart Save per turn.

- ☐ **Ultimate: Overtime Pep Rally** – *1/Long Rest*  
  As an action, all allies within 30 ft:  
  - Heal 2d6 HP  
  - Regain 1 EP  
  - Remove one Condition (GM-approved)  
  You may immediately take a bonus action to use Motivational Shout again for free.
